# RuneQuill Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

RuneQuill ("the app") is a tabletop RPG companion made by Nat1 Interactive ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

## The short version

- RuneQuill currently has no accounts and doesn't ask for your name, email, or a sign-in.
- Your characters, campaigns, notes, dice history and homebrew stay on your device. We currently don't run any servers, so we don't receive that content.
- The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers to serve and measure ads.
- If you buy "Remove Ads", Google Play handles the payment. We never see your card details.

## Information stored on your device

The app saves your campaigns, characters, character blueprints, notes, dice roll history, dice macros and Hub/homebrew entries in a local database on your phone. It also stores your theme preference and, if you've bought it, your Remove Ads status. Only you can access this data. We can't access it at all.

**Device backup.** If you've turned on your phone's own backup (for example Google backup on Android), your operating system may include RuneQuill's local database (campaigns, characters, notes, and other saved content) in that backup and in device-to-device transfers. The Remove Ads purchase status is excluded from device backup. Any backup of the local database goes to your own account with your phone's provider, under their privacy policy, and not to us.

**Exports and sharing.** When you choose to export a campaign or homebrew PDF, the app creates the file and opens your phone's share menu. Where it goes from there (email, Drive, a chat app, and so on) is up to you and is covered by that service's policy.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show, limit, and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

- your device's advertising ID (on Android, the Google Advertising ID)
- device and app information, such as model, OS version, language and app version
- your IP address and approximate location derived from it
- ad interactions, such as views and taps

Google's handling of this data is described in [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

**Your choices:**

- You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's settings (on Android: Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).
- Buying Remove Ads turns ads off.

## Purchases

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment and tells the app only whether the purchase is valid. We don't receive your name, card or billing address. See the [Google Payments Privacy Notice](https://payments.google.com/payments/apis-secure/get_legal_document?ldo=0&ldt=privacynotice).

## Data security

App data stays in the app's private storage on your device. The Remove Ads purchase status is stored encrypted (EncryptedSharedPreferences, AES-256) and is excluded from device backup. Ad data is sent to Google over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

## What we don't do

- We don't sell your personal information.
- We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services.
- We don't collect precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.

## Children

RuneQuill is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that concerns you, contact us below.

## Deleting your data

All of your RuneQuill data lives on your device. To delete it, use the app's delete options, clear the app's storage in your phone's settings, or uninstall the app. To delete a cloud copy, remove it from your phone's backup settings. Advertising data held by Google can be managed in your [Google account ad settings](https://myadcenter.google.com/).

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU or UK, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of your personal information. Since we don't hold any personal data on our side, most requests are handled by the steps above or by Google. You can still contact us with any request.

## Changes

We plan to add online features, such as sharing homebrew with other players. Before any feature like that collects new information (for example an account, a username, or content you upload), we'll update this policy to explain what's collected and why. If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date at the top and post the new version at this same address.
